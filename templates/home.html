For funded & prop traders

Most prop firms close inactive accounts within 30 days.

Whether you're trading a FTMO, FundedNext, MyForexFunds-style evaluation, or a funded account, an inactivity clause is a common rule. Here's how most firms handle it:

  • 📌 No trade for 30 days — account flagged / risks closure.
  • 📌 Some firms are stricter — 14 or 21 days.
  • 📌 A single trade can reset the inactivity counter.

FAIR tracks every account you add and reminds you before you hit that cutoff — so you always keep your funding active.
