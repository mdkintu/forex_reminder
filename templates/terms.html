What FAIR is

FAIR is a reminder tool. It tracks the last-trade date you enter for each trading account and sends you email, WhatsApp and/or Telegram reminders on a configurable schedule, so you can act before an inactivity clause affects your account.

Not a guarantee

FAIR is a convenience tool, not a substitute for checking your broker's or prop firm's own inactivity rules directly. Reminder delivery depends on third-party services (email providers, Twilio, Telegram) that are outside our control and can occasionally be delayed or fail. You remain solely responsible for keeping your trading accounts active and for verifying the actual inactivity policy that applies to each account.
