Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page is a plain-language starting point, not a substitute for review by a qualified lawyer. Before relying on it for a live product handling real users' data, have it reviewed for your jurisdiction.

What we collect

To run your inactivity reminders, FAIR stores:

  • Your email address (required to sign in).
  • Your name and Telegram chat ID, if you choose to add them — used only to address and deliver reminders.
  • Your browser-detected timezone, so reminders line up with your local calendar day.
  • The trading accounts you add: account name, account number, broker name, last-trade date/time, and which reminder channels you've enabled.
  • A log of reminders sent (date, channel, status), so you can see your own history and we don't send duplicates.

What we don't collect

  • We never ask for or store your broker login, password, or trading platform credentials.
  • We don't collect payment card details — FAIR does not process payments.
  • We don't run third-party advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data. We share the minimum needed to deliver a reminder with the service you asked for that reminder to go through:

  • Email — sent via our configured SMTP provider.
  • Telegram — sent via the Telegram Bot API, using the chat ID on your profile.

Each of those services only receives the message text and the contact detail needed to deliver it.

Cookies

FAIR uses only the functional cookies Django needs to keep you signed in and to protect forms from cross-site request forgery. No third-party tracking or advertising cookies are set.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and trading-account data for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, or to ask what we hold about you, see our Contact page.

Security

Passwords are stored using Django's built-in salted hashing — we never see or store your plaintext password. In production the site is served over HTTPS with secure, HTTP-only session cookies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us via the Contact page.
