MT5 auto-sync

The FAIR Sync Expert Advisor records each new trade's open time automatically, so the countdown resets without tapping “I traded”. It only reads your trade history: it can't place, change or close trades.

  1. On the computer you trade from (or another one on the same home network), open a separate MT5 and log in with the account's investor (read-only) password. One MT5 per account. Use your own computer, not a VPS: some firms ban or flag VPS logins. On Linux or Mac, MT5 runs under Wine.
  2. In that MT5: Tools › Options › Expert Advisors, tick “Allow WebRequest for listed URL” and add {{ site_url }}. Leave Algo Trading off.
  3. Download the EA below, open it in MetaEditor, compile (F7), then drag FAIR_Sync onto any chart.
  4. Make sure each account's Account number on FAIR is its MT5 login.

Check your firm's rules first. FundedNext CFD accounts of $50k and above ban EAs and “any automated tools”: ask FundedNext in writing before using FAIR Sync there, and use “I traded” until they say yes. Goat Funded Trader allows only EAs you wrote yourself and bans VPNs and VPSs, so run it only on your own computer. Never attach FAIR Sync to the MT5 you trade in.
